About Us

A Legacy of Trust & Excellence

Founded in 2016 by Abdul Waheed Meo, Al Waheed Group of Companies is a Karachi business group working across real estate development, construction, sales, marketing and investment. For 10 years we have helped families and investors buy property with confidence.

We earned our name as an authorized dealer for Al Ghafoor Builders & Developers and Falaknaz Group. Today we build our own, United Palm Greens in Scheme 43, and proudly sponsor Khairunnisa Heights in Scheme 33.

  • Authorized dealer for Falaknaz Group since 2023

FAQs

Questions Buyers Ask Us

Can't find your answer? Our advisors are happy to help on WhatsApp during office hours.

WhatsApp an Advisor

Where are Al Waheed Group projects located?

United Palm Greens is on the Main 400 ft Wide Road in Scheme 43, Karachi, beside Silk Garden by Naya Nazimabad. Khairunnisa Heights, which we sponsor, is on Main Scheme 33 Road, Punjabi Sodagran, Karachi.

What apartments are available at Khairunnisa Heights?

Khairunnisa Heights offers Ruby and Opal 4 room apartments and Diamond 5 room apartments, on a plan of two down payments, 24 monthly and 4 half yearly installments and a payment on possession.

What is the payment plan for United Palm Greens?

Booking starts from Rs. 600,000, and the balance is paid in easy monthly and half yearly installments. You can view and download the full payment schedule on the project page.

Can overseas Pakistanis book a plot remotely?

Yes. We handle the full booking remotely, with virtual site tours, digital documentation and an advisor available on WhatsApp across time zones.

What plot sizes are available?

United Palm Greens offers residential plots of 120, 400 and 2,000 square yards, plus commercial plots at the entrance.

How do I book a site visit?

Book a free consultation through this website, call us or send a WhatsApp message. Our team will arrange a guided site visit at a time that suits you.

Is Al Waheed Group an authorized dealer?

Yes. Al Waheed Group was an authorized dealer for Al Ghafoor Builders & Developers from 2022 to 2023 and has been an authorized dealer for Falaknaz Group since 2023.
